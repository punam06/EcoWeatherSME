# EcoWeatherSME: 3-Minute Video Pitch Script
## Vibe to Production in 180 Seconds

**Target pace:** ~145 words/minute (~435 words total)  
**Total duration:** **180 seconds exact**

---

## Segment 1 — Problem (0:00–0:30)
**Key message:** *This problem matters*  
**Tone:** Urgent, human, data-grounded

**Speaker Script**
“Every monsoon, farmers in Bangladesh lose value from bio-waste they could convert into income. Coconut husk, rice husk, and crop residue spoil before they reach processors or buyers.

Today, 40% of agricultural bio-waste in South Asia is unused, and soil stress affects 1.5 billion people globally. The core problem is uncertainty: farmers, processors, logistics teams, and buyers cannot predict if shipments survive humidity, heat, and delay. When uncertainty wins, waste is burned, income is lost, and emissions rise.”

---

## Segment 2 — Solution (0:30–1:00)
**Key message:** *This is how we solve it*  
**Tone:** Clear, differentiated, confident

**Speaker Script**
“EcoWeatherSME is an AI-native platform for monsoon agriculture. We combine IoT sensors, LLM + RAG reasoning, and microclimate survival modeling to predict **asset survival**, not just weather.

Our engine gives every shipment a personalized risk score, recommends pre-treatment or rerouting, and creates blockchain traceability from farm to buyer. Farmers get decision clarity before loss happens.”

---

## Segment 3 — Demo / Concept Flow (1:00–2:00)
**Key message:** *This is how it works*  
**Tone:** Practical, stepwise, visual

**Speaker Script**
“Here is the workflow: **Farmer input → IoT integration → AI engine → Personalized output → Blockchain proof.**

Real scenario: A farmer near Dhaka has 500 kilograms of coconut husk and wants shipment to Chittagong during monsoon.

Step one: He enters batch type, weight, route, and delivery time.  
Step two: IoT data and weather forecasts stream in—humidity, temperature, pressure, rainfall risk.  
Step three: Our AI engine compares this case against historical shipments and agronomic knowledge.

Output: Survival probability is 72% as-is. Recommendation: pre-dry for 12 hours or improve ventilation. With pre-drying, survival rises to 91%. The system suggests the best conversion pathway and expected carbon-credit yield.

The farmer confirms, shipment metadata is written to blockchain, and buyer-side verification closes payment automatically after delivery conditions are met.”

---

## Segment 4 — AI Approach (2:00–2:30)
**Key message:** *This is real AI thinking*  
**Tone:** Technical but accessible

**Speaker Script**
“Our stack has four layers: fine-tuned LLM, RAG retrieval, microclimate regression, and a supply-chain knowledge graph.

Training foundation: 500+ case studies and 2,000+ real shipment records. Current survival prediction accuracy is 87% on validation data.

Personalization uses user clustering and a multi-armed bandit feedback loop so recommendations improve by behavior and region. We also enforce ethical AI with bias checks, explainable reasoning, fairness audits, and Bangla-first localization.”

---

## Segment 5 — Impact & Next Steps (2:30–3:00)
**Key message:** *We can build and scale this*  
**Tone:** Ambitious, measurable, grounded

**Speaker Script**
“Our target outcomes are clear: 40% spoilage reduction, 30% premium pricing uplift, and $2,000 to $5,000 extra annual income per farmer.

At ecosystem scale, this enables 500,000 tons of bio-waste conversion yearly and up to 250,000 tons of CO₂-credit value, starting with 10,000+ farmers in Bangladesh.

Business model: SaaS subscriptions, transaction fees, and data partnerships, targeting 60% gross margin.

Roadmap: Phase 1, Q2–Q3 2026—100-farmer pilot. Phase 2, Q4 2026–Q2 2027—1,000 farmers and $50K MRR. Phase 3, 2027+—100K+ users.

To accelerate, we need $250K seed, NRB partnership support, and technical co-founders. **EcoWeatherSME turns climate risk into farmer income. Let’s build it.**”

## Speaker Notes & Delivery Guidance

### Timing Control
- 0:00–0:30 Problem
- 0:30–1:00 Solution
- 1:00–2:00 Demo
- 2:00–2:30 AI approach
- 2:30–3:00 Impact + CTA

### Bangla Localization Tips
- Open or close with one respectful Bangla line: **“আমাদের লক্ষ্য, কৃষকের ঝুঁকি কমিয়ে আয় বাড়ানো।”**
- Keep product terms bilingual when needed: “risk score (ঝুঁকি স্কোর)”, “shipment proof (চালান প্রমাণ)”.
- Use local place names clearly: Dhaka, Chittagong, monsoon routes.

### Tone Cues
- Problem: empathetic urgency
- Solution: clarity over jargon
- Demo: crisp, concrete, testable
- AI: structured confidence
- Closing: hopeful but financially grounded

- [ ] One-screen flow graphic visible during demo segment
- [ ] AI terms explained in plain language (LLM, RAG, regression)
- [ ] Bangladesh context repeated in problem, demo, and impact
- [ ] Ethical AI line delivered clearly (bias, explainability, fairness)
