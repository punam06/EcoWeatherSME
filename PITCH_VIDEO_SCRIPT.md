# EcoWeatherSME: 3-Minute Video Pitch Script
## Vibe to Production in 180 Seconds

**Target pace:** ~145 words/minute (~435 words total)  
**Total duration:** **180 seconds exact**

---

## Segment 1 — Problem (0:00–0:30)
**Key message:** *This problem matters*  
**Tone:** Urgent, human, data-grounded

**Speaker Script**
"Climate volatility—particularly heat islands and extreme microclimatic fluctuations—is destroying value for SMEs managing temperature-sensitive products. Pharmaceutical shipments, organic produce, dairy, and bio-waste processors lose inventory when conditions shift unexpectedly.

In South Asia, monsoon seasons combined with urban heat islands create 20–30% spoilage risk. Globally, 1.5 billion people depend on supply chains that lack real-time microclimate survival insight. The core problem is uncertainty: SME operators ship blind, hoping conditions hold—and often lose their margin to climate volatility."

---

## Segment 2 — Solution (0:30–1:00)
**Key message:** *This is how we solve it*  
**Tone:** Clear, differentiated, confident

**Speaker Script**
"EcoWeatherSME is an AI-native platform for climate-resilient supply chains. We combine IoT sensors, agentic AI workflows, and microclimate survival modeling to predict **asset survival**, not just weather forecasts.

Our engine gives every shipment a personalized risk score, recommends pre-treatment or rerouting, and provides compliance verification through BSTI and trusted standards—creating transparent, verifiable proof from origin to buyer. SME operators get decision clarity before loss happens.

We're also launching a **marketplace** where buyers can discover verified, climate-resilient shipments—and SMEs can access tailored, custom services designed for their specific product and route."

---

## Segment 3 — Demo / Concept Flow (1:00–2:00)
**Key message:** *This is how it works*  
**Tone:** Practical, stepwise, visual

**Speaker Script**
"Here is the workflow: **SME input → IoT integration → Agentic AI reasoning → Personalized output → Standards verification.**

Real scenario: An SME processor has 500 kilograms of temperature-sensitive goods and wants to ship during monsoon season.

Step one: They enter product type, weight, route, and delivery window—or select from our **custom SME services** menu tailored to their business.  
Step two: IoT data and hyperlocal weather forecasts stream in—humidity, temperature, heat island effects, pressure, rainfall risk.  
Step three: Our agentic AI engine reasons through this shipment against historical cases and domain knowledge, autonomously recommending interventions.

Output: Survival probability is 72% as-is. Recommendation: add thermal packaging or adjust transport timing. With intervention, survival rises to 91%. The system suggests the best pathway and cost tradeoffs.

The operator confirms, shipment metadata is verified against BSTI or partner standards, and buyer-side verification closes payment automatically once delivery conditions are verified. Both parties see transparent proof of compliance."

**Key message:** *Early validation, sustainable growth*  
**Tone:** Grounded, focused, honest

**Speaker Script**

We're building a sustainable model: SaaS subscriptions, per-shipment risk scoring, marketplace commissions, and custom SME service packages. Gross margin target is 50–60%.

Our immediate roadmap includes piloting with SME operators across Bangladesh and South Asia, expanding marketplace adoption with buyers, integrating advanced features like messaging integration and route optimization, and scaling our marketplace to connect climate-resilient supply chains at scale.

To accelerate this, we're seeking funding, technical co-founder support, and strategic partnerships in logistics, standards verification, and marketplace growth.

EcoWeatherSME turns climate risk into competitive advantage for SMEs. Let's build it together."

## Speaker Notes & Delivery Guidance

### Timing Control
- 0:00–0:30 Problem (heat islands + monsoon volatility + all SMEs with heat-sensitive goods)
- 0:30–1:00 Solution (AI-native platform + standards verification + marketplace)
- 1:00–2:00 Demo (generic SME operator scenario + custom services)
- 2:00–2:30 AI approach + ESG impact display
- 2:30–3:00 Impact + focused roadmap + CTA

### Product Scope Emphasis
- Open with "SMEs managing heat-sensitive products" in regions with microclimate volatility
- Emphasize **heat islands + monsoon** as key climate drivers (not just monsoon)
- Demo uses generic "operator" language (not farmer-specific)
- Mention key verticals: pharmaceuticals, produce, dairy, bio-waste
- Highlight **marketplace** and **custom SME services** as differentiators
- Explain standards verification (BSTI, trusted partners) as proof mechanism—not blockchain

### Technology Stack Clarity
- Agentic AI workflows powered by LLM APIs (Grok)
- RAG retrieval over domain knowledge and case studies
- Standards-based verification (BSTI, partner networks)
- No blockchain claims; focus on transparent, verifiable compliance

### Marketplace & Custom Services
- Live or high-fidelity mockup showing marketplace UI
- Highlight how SMEs discover services, buyers find verified shipments
- Show custom SME service packages tailored by product/region

### ESG Impact Display (2:00–2:30)
- Live or high-fidelity mockup of web app ESG score visible on screen
- Show: spoilage %, CO₂ saved, income uplift, trust score, verification status
- Narrate what these metrics mean for the business case

### Bangla Localization Tips
- Open or close with one respectful Bangla line: **"জলবায়ু ঝুঁকি কমিয়ে ব্যবসায়ীদের আয় বাড়ান।"** (Reduce climate risk, increase SME income.)
- Keep product terms bilingual: "risk score (ঝুঁকি স্কোর)", "survival proof (সার্ভাইভাল প্রমাণ)", "marketplace (বাজার)"
- Use local context: heat islands, monsoon, South Asia, Bangladesh—but keep product industry-agnostic

### Tone Cues
- Problem: empathetic urgency (all SMEs at heat-island and climate volatility risk)
- Solution: clarity, technical credibility, standards-grounded
- Demo: concrete but operator-generic; emphasize custom services
- AI + ESG: tangible metrics, verified compliance, marketplace trust
- Closing: focused on near-term pilots and feature expansion—avoid over-promising on scale
