# EcoWeatherSME: 3-Minute Video Pitch Script
## Vibe to Production in 180 Seconds

**Target pace:** ~145 words/minute (~435 words total)  
**Total duration:** **180 seconds exact**

---

## Segment 1 — Problem (0:00–0:30)
**Key message:** *This problem matters*  
**Tone:** Urgent, human, data-grounded

**Speaker Script**
"Climate volatility is destroying value for SMEs managing heat-sensitive products. Pharmaceutical shipments, organic produce, dairy, and bio-waste processors lose inventory when microclimate conditions shift unexpectedly.

In South Asia, monsoon seasons create 20–30% spoilage risk. Globally, 1.5 billion people depend on supply chains that lack real-time asset survival insight. The core problem is uncertainty: SME operators, logistics partners, and buyers can't predict loss before it happens."

---

## Segment 2 — Solution (0:30–1:00)
**Key message:** *This is how we solve it*  
**Tone:** Clear, differentiated, confident

**Speaker Script**
"EcoWeatherSME is an AI-native platform for climate-resilient supply chains. We combine IoT sensors, LLM + RAG reasoning, and microclimate survival modeling to predict **asset survival**, not just weather.

Our engine gives every shipment a personalized risk score, recommends pre-treatment or rerouting, and creates blockchain traceability from origin to buyer. SME operators get decision clarity before loss happens—whether you're shipping pharmaceuticals, produce, dairy, or bio-waste."

---

## Segment 3 — Demo / Concept Flow (1:00–2:00)
**Key message:** *This is how it works*  
**Tone:** Practical, stepwise, visual

**Speaker Script**
"Here is the workflow: **SME input → IoT integration → AI engine → Personalized output → Blockchain proof.**

Real scenario: An SME processor has 500 kilograms of temperature-sensitive goods and wants to ship during monsoon season.

Step one: They enter product type, weight, route, and delivery window.  
Step two: IoT data and weather forecasts stream in—humidity, temperature, pressure, rainfall risk.  
Step three: Our AI engine compares this shipment against historical cases and domain knowledge.

Output: Survival probability is 72% as-is. Recommendation: add thermal packaging or adjust transport timing. With intervention, survival rises to 91%. The system suggests the best pathway and cost trade-offs.

The operator confirms, shipment metadata is written to blockchain, and buyer-side verification closes payment automatically once delivery conditions are verified."

---

**Key message:** *Early validation, sustainable growth*  
**Tone:** Grounded, focused, honest

EcoWeatherSME turns climate risk into competitive advantage for SMEs. Let's build it together."

## Speaker Notes & Delivery Guidance

### Timing Control
- 0:00–0:30 Problem (all SMEs, heat-sensitive goods)
- 0:30–1:00 Solution
- 1:00–2:00 Demo (generic SME operator scenario)
- 2:00–2:30 AI + ESG impact display
- 2:30–3:00 Impact + realistic roadmap + CTA

### Product Scope Emphasis
- Open with "SMEs managing heat-sensitive products"
- Demo uses generic "operator" language (not farmer-specific)
- Mention key verticals: pharmaceuticals, produce, dairy, bio-waste
- Keep it industry-agnostic while grounded in South Asian context

### ESG Impact Display (2:00–2:30)
- Live or high-fidelity mockup of web app ESG score visible on screen
- Show: spoilage %, CO₂ saved, income uplift, trust score
- Narrate what these metrics mean for the business case

### Bangla Localization Tips
- Open or close with one respectful Bangla line: **"জলবায়ু ঝুঁকি কমিয়ে ব্যবসায়ীদের আয় বাড়ান।"** (Reduce climate risk, grow business income)
- Keep product terms bilingual: "risk score (ঝুঁকি স্কোর)", "survival proof (সার্ভাইভাল প্রমাণ)"
- Use local context: monsoon, South Asia, Bangladesh—but don't lock product to one segment

### Tone Cues
- Problem: empathetic urgency (all SMEs at risk)
- Solution: clarity, industry-agnostic language
- Demo: concrete but operator-generic
- AI + ESG: tangible metrics and verification
- Closing: honest about pilot stage, focused on next steps

### Delivery Checklist
- [ ] Timed run lands at 2:55–3:00 with natural pauses
- [ ] Product scope is SMEs + heat-sensitive goods (not farmers only)
- [ ] Demo uses operator language, not farmer-specific details
- [ ] ESG impact score visible and narrated during segment 4
- [ ] Roadmap claims are conservative (30–40% spoilage, 200+ SMEs, 92%+ accuracy targets)
- [ ] Final ask is specific: $250K, technical co-founders, strategic partnerships
- [ ] No overpromising on timeline or scale
