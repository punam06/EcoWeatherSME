## SLIDE 6: AI ARCHITECTURE (The Technical Depth)
### Visual
- **Technical stack diagram**:
  - Layer 1: LLM (fine-tuned on agronomic data) + RAG (retrieval from 50K+ documents)
  - Layer 2: Microclimate model (87% accuracy, 2K+ training journeys)
  - Layer 3: Knowledge graph (bio-assets, conditions, pathways, buyers)
  - Layer 4: Personalization engine (multi-armed bandit, user clustering)
  - Layer 5: Blockchain & APIs
- **Table**: Model performance metrics (accuracy, latency, fairness)

### Verbal (70–100 sec)
"Here's the AI thinking:

**LLM + RAG Architecture:**
Our language model is fine-tuned on 500+ agricultural case studies and 10,000+ microclimate scenarios. When a farmer asks 'Can I ferment rice husks in monsoon?', the system retrieves relevant research papers, historical data, and best practices from our vector database—then synthesizes a personalized, explainable answer.

**Microclimate Survival Model:**
We built a multi-variable regression: Temperature × Humidity × Pressure × Asset_Type = Spoilage_Risk. Trained on 2,000+ real Bangladesh shipping journeys. Accuracy: 87%. Updates every 5 minutes as IoT data streams in.

**Knowledge Graph:**
We've mapped bio-assets, environmental conditions, conversion pathways, and buyer requirements into a graph. Queries like 'Which conversion maximizes carbon credits AND meets organic certification?' are answered in milliseconds.

**Personalization:**
We cluster users by risk tolerance and asset type. A multi-armed bandit algorithm tests recommendations and learns what works for each cohort.

**Ethical AI:**
Every recommendation is explainable. We audit model fairness across farmer types. Farmers see how their data improves the system for their community."

## VISUAL DESIGN NOTES FOR SLIDES

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: Green (#2E7D32) — growth, sustainability
- **Secondary**: Blue (#1565C0) — trust, technology
- **Accent**: Orange (#F57C00) — energy, innovation
- **Neutral**: White/Light Gray — clarity

### Typography
- **Headlines**: Bold, sans-serif (Montserrat or similar)
- **Body**: Clean sans-serif (Open Sans, Inter)
- **Data/Code**: Monospace for technical specifics

### Media Recommendations
- **Slide 1**: Drone footage or wide landscape of Bangladesh rice fields
- **Slide 2**: Time-lapse of monsoon, split-screen of waste burning
- **Slide 3**: Persona cards with icons and names
- **Slide 5**: Animated flow diagram with live sensor data visualization
- **Slide 6**: Animated tech stack; floating model metrics
- **Slide 8**: Animated bar charts showing reduction % and income gains
- **Slide 9**: Animated timeline with milestones
- **Slide 10**: Montage of farmer faces, green fields, IoT sensors (30-sec loop behind speaker)

### Animation Timing
- Slides should NOT distract from the verbal message
- Introduce each element as you speak about it (entrance animations)
- Keep animations <2 sec per slide
- Total slide transition time: <1 minute for all 10 slides
