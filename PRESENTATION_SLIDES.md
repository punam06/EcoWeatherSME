## Slide 5 — Demo Flow (End-to-End)
### Visual Specifications
- Horizontal flow graphic: **Input → IoT → AI → Output → Blockchain**
- Scenario card: Dhaka farmer, 500kg coconut husk, Chittagong route, monsoon icon
- Output panel with 72% → 91% improvement

### Verbal Talking Points (1:00–2:00)
“Here is a live-use scenario. A Dhaka farmer enters 500 kilograms of coconut husk for shipment to Chittagong during monsoon.

The system ingests route details, IoT readings, and forecast signals. Our AI engine compares this case against past shipments and agronomic knowledge.

Result: survival probability is 72% as-is. Recommendation: pre-dry for 12 hours or improve container airflow. With pre-drying, survival increases to 91%. The engine also recommends the best conversion pathway and estimates carbon-credit potential.

After confirmation, shipment evidence is anchored on blockchain. On delivery, condition verification triggers trustable buyer acceptance and settlement.”

### Delivery Notes
- Use cursor/hand to follow the flow left-to-right
- Keep pacing steady; this is the core proof section
